# Sudoku+ Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026. Applies to Sudoku+ 1.3 and later (package `com.zsacic.sudoku`).

## The short version
Sudoku+ works entirely on your device. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics, and it never sends anything anywhere.

## Who is responsible
Tintako, Croatia. Contact: app@tintako.com.

## What the app stores, and where
Only on your device, in the app's private storage: the game in progress on each level (the numbers, notes and selected cell), your two game settings (mark repeats, highlight same numbers) and the language you chose. Nothing is copied to backups or moved to a new device by the app. Deleting a game or uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions, and what each is for
None. The app asks for no phone permissions. The Android system adds one internal permission (`DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`) that only protects the app's own internal messages and gives access to nothing on your device.

## What leaves, and only when you ask
Nothing. The Tip jar page shows a QR code and the address `ko-fi.com/tintako`; the app itself never opens it. Only another phone that scans the code goes to that page.

## Who else receives anything
No one. The app connects to no servers.

## Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)
The app processes no personal data on Tintako's behalf. What it keeps stays on your device under your control.

## Your rights
Because Tintako receives no data from the app, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete everything by deleting games or uninstalling the app. You can complain to your national data protection authority (in Croatia, AZOP).

## Children
Sudoku+ is suitable for everyone. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is published here and in the app (Settings → About → Privacy), with a new date.
